# EMORA business model

## Customer products
1. BASIC — template-driven one-off purchase.
2. AI — AI-assisted one-off purchase with more sections/functions.
3. PRO AI — full conversational AI website builder + premium hosting/domain options.

## Revenue streams
- One-off site creation
- Premium AI generation
- Custom domain
- Hosting renewal
- Premium templates
- Template marketplace commission
- Subscription for advanced AI credits
- Business/agency plan

## Viral loop
Every public site can contain a subtle “Made with EMORA” CTA.
Referral credits can reward both the inviter and new user.

## Core KPI
Track:
- visitor → Telegram/payment conversion
- payment → site creation
- site creation → publish
- published site → share
- shared site → new signup
- repeat purchase
- AI usage cost / customer revenue
